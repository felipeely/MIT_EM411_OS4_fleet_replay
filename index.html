    How to read it. Each dot in the first chart is one request, placed where it arrived and as high as its wait. The dashed line is the 5 minute requirement. Shaded columns show hourly demand. In the rush-hour day, tinted hours are the traffic hours, when shuttles run at 58% and bikes at 85% of normal speed. The simulation has no street map: it draws random trip lengths and random positions. For this picture each pickup, ride and empty return is placed on real streets with the same length and the same timing, so queues, battery stops and waits match the numbers in the model validation exactly. Streets, buildings and river are open data from the City of Cambridge GIS, and landmark positions are approximate.
